# POL-06: Branch and contact centre service standards
Owner: Retail Operations · Version 1.8 · Approved 01/02/2026

## §1 Waiting times
§1.1 Target branch queue time is 10 minutes. Waits over 30 minutes must be recorded by the branch manager.

## §2 Conduct
§2.1 Staff must treat customers courteously. Conduct complaints are passed to the branch or team manager within 2 business days.

## §3 Call-backs
§3.1 Promised call-backs must happen within the time agreed. A missed call-back is a service failure and should be recorded with root cause code SVC-CB.
