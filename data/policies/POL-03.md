# POL-03: Overdrafts and account fees
Owner: Current Accounts · Version 2.4 · Approved 20/01/2026

## §1 Overdraft charges
§1.1 Arranged overdrafts are charged at a single annual interest rate. There are no fixed daily or monthly overdraft fees.
§1.2 Unpaid item fees are £0. Returned payments must not create a fee.

## §2 Fee refunds
§2.1 Refund interest and charges caused by a bank error in full.
§2.2 Handlers may refund up to 3 months of overdraft interest as a goodwill gesture where a customer is in financial difficulty, within POL-05 limits.

## §3 Financial difficulty
§3.1 Where a customer shows signs of financial difficulty, offer a referral to the Financial Support team and pause collection activity for 30 days.
