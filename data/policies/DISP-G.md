# DISP-G: Regulatory complaint rules (internal summary)
Owner: Complaints Governance · Version 2.0 · Approved 01/03/2026

## §1 Definitions
§1.1 A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, oral or written, justified or not, about the bank's service that alleges the customer has suffered, or may suffer, financial loss, material distress or material inconvenience.
§1.2 Complaints are recorded on the day received, whatever channel they arrive through (phone, branch, app, email, letter or social media).

## §2 Timescales
§2.1 Complaints resolved by close of business on the third business day after receipt may be closed with a Summary Resolution Communication (SRC).
§2.2 All other complaints must receive a final response within 8 weeks (56 calendar days) of receipt, or a written explanation of why the bank cannot yet respond.
§2.3 Every SRC and final response must tell the customer they may refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS), and include the FOS contact details.
§2.4 Complaints about payment services (card payments, transfers, direct debits) must receive a final response within 15 business days. In exceptional circumstances outside the bank's control, a holding reply must explain the delay and a final response must follow within 35 business days.
§2.5 The customer has 6 months from the date of the final response to refer to the FOS. The final response must state this.

## §3 Fair handling
§3.1 Complaints must be investigated competently, diligently and impartially.
§3.2 Responses must be fair, clear and not misleading, and written in plain language.
§3.3 Root causes must be recorded so recurring problems can be fixed (see POL-01 §5).
