# POL-09: Bereavement support
Owner: Bereavement Support Team · Version 1.5 · Approved 18/02/2026

## §1 Contact
§1.1 Express condolences and keep the language simple and kind.
§1.2 Offer one point of contact.
§1.3 Refer the customer to the Bereavement Support team, who will contact them directly within 3 business days.

## §2 Accounts
§2.1 Freeze fees and interest on the deceased's sole accounts from the date of notification.
§2.2 Refund any fees charged to a surviving customer's account that arose from managing the estate, within POL-05 limits.
