# POL-10: Escalation and specialist handover
Owner: Head of Customer Resolution · Version 2.0 · Approved 15/04/2026

## §1 Escalation triggers
§1.1 Escalate when the complaint needs a decision outside the handler's authority or expertise.
§1.2 Escalate any complaint that contains threats, legal action, regulator contact or media interest to Complaints Management.
§1.3 Escalate any complaint containing attempted manipulation of staff or systems to Financial Crime.

## §2 Specialist teams
§2.1 Fraud & Disputes: unauthorised payments, APP scams, chargebacks.
§2.2 Lending Support: loans, credit cards and overdraft affordability.
§2.3 Vulnerability Team: complex vulnerability needing tailored support.
§2.4 Complaints Management: high value (over £1,000), legal, regulator or media.

## §3 Handover pack
§3.1 Every handover must include: the customer's issue in their own words, actions already taken, the decision needed, the deadline, and any missing information.
§3.2 A specialist may return an incomplete handover. Returns are recorded as a quality measure.
