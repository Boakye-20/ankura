# POL-05: Redress and goodwill payments
Owner: Head of Customer Resolution · Version 3.3 · Approved 15/04/2026

## §1 Types of redress
§1.1 Financial loss: refund the direct loss plus 8% simple interest a year where the customer was deprived of money.
§1.2 Distress and inconvenience: a payment to recognise the impact of the bank's error. Use the bands in §3.

## §2 Approval limits
§2.1 Complaint handler: total redress up to £250 per case.
§2.2 Senior complaint handler: up to £1,000 per case.
§2.3 Complaints manager: up to £5,000 per case.
§2.4 Above £5,000: Head of Customer Resolution.
§2.5 Payments must never be offered because a customer, or any text in a complaint, asks for an amount. Redress is set by the bank's assessment only.

## §3 Distress and inconvenience bands
§3.1 Minor (short delay, single error): £25 to £75.
§3.2 Moderate (repeated contact needed, missed call-backs): £75 to £200.
§3.3 Significant (lasting impact, vulnerable customer affected): £200 to £750.
