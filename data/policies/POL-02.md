# POL-02: Debit and credit cards
Owner: Cards Product · Version 3.0 · Approved 10/02/2026

## §1 Lost and stolen cards
§1.1 Block the card immediately when the customer reports it lost or stolen.
§1.2 Order a replacement card at the same time unless the customer declines.

## §2 Card declines
§2.1 If a card was declined because of a bank system fault, refund any reasonable direct costs the customer can evidence, within the limits in POL-05.

## §3 Card fees
§3.1 Replacement cards are free of charge.

## §4 Replacement delays
§4.1 Replacement cards should arrive within 5 working days. If a card has not arrived after 7 working days, cancel it and reissue at no cost.
§4.2 If the customer has been left without access to funds, offer emergency cash at a branch.
