# POL-01: Complaints handling procedure
Owner: Head of Customer Resolution · Version 4.1 · Approved 15/04/2026

## §1 Receiving a complaint
§1.1 Log every complaint on the case system on the day received, with channel, product and the customer's own words.
§1.2 Acknowledge written complaints within 1 business day unless they are resolved within that time.
§1.3 Check for signs of vulnerability at first contact and record them (see POL-07).

## §2 Triage
§2.1 Assign a product category and an issue category from the approved taxonomy.
§2.2 Set the deadline clock using DISP-G §2.1, §2.2 or §2.4.
§2.3 Route to a specialist team using POL-10 when the case meets an escalation trigger.

## §3 Investigation
§3.1 Review all case notes, call records and account history before reaching a decision.
§3.2 Contact the customer if information is missing; do not assume.

## §4 Resolution
§4.1 Decide whether the complaint is upheld, partly upheld or not upheld, and record the reason.
§4.2 Where the bank got something wrong, put the customer back in the position they would have been in (see POL-05).
§4.3 Final responses need approval at the level set in POL-05 §2.

## §5 Learning
§5.1 Record a root cause code on every closed case.
§5.2 Complaints Governance reviews root cause trends monthly.

## §6 Use of AI assistance
§6.1 AI tools may summarise, classify and draft, but every outbound response must be approved by a named member of staff.
§6.2 Staff must not paste unmasked personal data into any AI tool that is not approved under POL-08.
