# POL-07: Customers in vulnerable circumstances
Owner: Customer Vulnerability Lead · Version 2.6 · Approved 12/03/2026

## §1 Recognising vulnerability
§1.1 A vulnerable customer is someone who, due to their personal circumstances, is especially susceptible to harm, particularly when the bank does not act with appropriate care.
§1.2 Drivers include poor health, life events (such as bereavement, job loss or relationship breakdown), low financial resilience and low capability (such as limited digital skills or language barriers).
§1.3 Customers do not need to use the word "vulnerable". Staff should act on indirect signs.

## §2 Handling
§2.1 Record vulnerability on an appropriate lawful basis, tell the customer how the information will be used, and record only the detail needed.
§2.2 Complaints with a vulnerability flag need co-approval by a senior complaint handler before the final response is sent.
§2.3 Offer adjustments such as a named contact, a preferred channel or more time.

## §3 Fees and charges
§3.1 Pause fees and collections for 30 days while support is arranged.
§3.2 Refund fees and charges incurred because of the circumstances that made the customer vulnerable, where the bank knew or should reasonably have known about them.
