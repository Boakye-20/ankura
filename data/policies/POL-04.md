# POL-04: Unauthorised transactions and fraud
Owner: Fraud & Disputes · Version 5.2 · Approved 05/05/2026

## §1 Scope
§1.1 This policy covers card and account payments the customer says they did not authorise, and authorised push payment (APP) scams.

## §2 Unauthorised payments
§2.1 Refund an unauthorised payment as soon as possible and no later than the end of the next business day after it is reported, unless there are reasonable grounds to suspect fraud by the customer. Those grounds must be recorded and reported to Financial Crime.
§2.2 The customer may be liable for up to £35 if the payment resulted from a lost or stolen card, unless the loss could not have been detected by the customer.
§2.3 The customer is not refunded where they acted fraudulently or with gross negligence. Only Fraud & Disputes may decide gross negligence.

## §3 Handling
§3.1 Block the card or payment method immediately.
§3.2 Raise a chargeback with the card scheme where appropriate.
§3.3 Complaints handlers must not decide whether a payment was authorised. Route to Fraud & Disputes (POL-10 §2.1).
§3.4 Fraud & Disputes will contact the customer within 2 business days of receiving the handover.

## §4 APP scams
§4.1 Eligible APP scam claims are reimbursed up to the scheme maximum, less any excess the bank applies, unless an exception such as gross negligence applies.
§4.2 Check for vulnerability. Vulnerable customers are not subject to the gross negligence exception or the excess.
