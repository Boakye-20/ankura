# POL-08: Complaint data protection
Owner: Data Protection Officer · Version 2.1 · Approved 30/04/2026

## §1 Data minimisation
§1.1 Only the personal data needed to resolve the complaint may be processed.
§1.2 Before complaint text is sent to an approved AI service, names, account numbers, sort codes, card numbers, email addresses, phone numbers and postal addresses must be replaced with tokens.
§1.3 The token map stays inside the bank's systems and is applied only when a member of staff views the case.

## §2 Special category data
§2.1 Health information disclosed in a complaint is special category data. Record only what is needed to support the customer.

## §3 Retention and audit
§3.1 Complaint records, including AI outputs and staff decisions, are kept for 6 years after closure.
§3.2 Every AI-generated output must be logged with the model, prompt version, sources used and the member of staff who acted on it.
