# 12 · Eight-week letter and payment services holding reply (verified 09/11/2026)

Checked against the FCA Handbook on 09/11/2026 (DISP 1.6 last updated 01/06/2026; a future version dated 25/10/2027 was not reviewed). Not legal advice.

## Summary
- Acknowledge promptly in writing and keep the customer updated (DISP 1.6.1R).
- By the end of eight weeks, send a final response or a "not yet" letter (DISP 1.6.2R) that explains the delay, gives an expected date, and signposts the Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS) with its leaflet and website.
- The six-month wording (DISP 1 Annex 3R) belongs in the final response only. An eight-week letter does not start the six-month clock (DISP 2.8).
- Payment services and e-money: final response within 15 business days; in exceptional circumstances, a holding response by day 15 with reasons and a deadline, then a final response by day 35 (DISP 1.6.2AR).

## Required content of the letter
Eight-week letter (DISP 1.6.2R(2)):
- EXPLAINS_DELAY: why no final response yet · Required
- EXPECTED_DATE: when a final response is expected · Required
- FOS_REFER_NOW: customer may now refer to the FOS · Required
- FOS_LEAFLET: FOS leaflet enclosed · Required
- FOS_WEBSITE: FOS website address · Required
- WAIVER_IF_OUT_OF_TIME: waiver position, only if complaint is out of time · Required (conditional)
- NO_SIX_MONTH_CLOCK: no "six months from this letter" wording · Required

Holding response (DISP 1.6.2AR(2)(a)):
- SENT_BY_DAY_15 · Payment services only
- DELAY_REASONS: clear reasons for the delay · Payment services only
- FINAL_DEADLINE: date of final response, no later than business day 35 · Payment services only

Final response:
- OUTCOME: accept, offer redress, or reject with reasons · Final response only
- ANNEX3_WORDING: six months from the letter's date · Final response only
- DURABLE_MEDIUM: paper or agreed durable medium · Payment services only

## How this maps to the synthetic policy
- **DISP-G §2.2** asks only why the bank "cannot yet respond". It omits the expected date, FOS referral, leaflet and website, and §2.3 signposts the FOS only in SRCs and final responses. Its "56 calendar days" is the bank's reading; the Handbook gives no day count.
- **§2.3 "FOS contact details"** is looser than the rule (leaflet plus website).
- **§2.4** matches 15/35 business days. It leaves out the day-15 deadline for the holding reply, the named final deadline, and the durable medium. The rule covers PSD complaints (PSRs Parts 6 and 7) and EMD complaints. Card payments, transfers and direct debits are only examples.
- **§2.5** matches Annex 3R and DISP 2.8, provided it is limited to the final response.
